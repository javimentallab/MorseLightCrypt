# Privacy policy — MorseLightCrypt

*Last updated: 8 November 2026*

*Versión en español: [PRIVACY.es.md](PRIVACY.es.md)*

## 1. Data controller

The MorseLightCrypt application (the "App") is published on Google Play by Javier Comyn Pérez, with address at calle Olivos, número 36, 28670 Villaviciosa de Odón, Madrid, España (the "Provider"), under the developer name javimentallab. The Provider is the controller of the personal data referred to in this policy, and the User may contact the Provider at jotacomyn@gmail.com.

## 2. No processing of personal data in the App

The App does not collect or process any personal data of the User. The App has no user accounts, advertising, analytics tools or crash reporting systems, and it does not request the Internet access permission, so it cannot transmit data to third parties. The only personal data processed by the Provider are those relating to the purchase of the App, described in section 6.

## 3. Permissions

- **Camera:** used solely while the Receive or Decrypt tab is open in light mode, including the period before reception starts, so that the User can aim the device. Images are analysed on the device itself as they are captured, are neither stored nor transmitted, and no person has access to them.
- **Microphone:** used solely while Morse messages are being received by sound, and active only while the App is listening. The audio is analysed on the device itself and is not recorded, stored or transmitted.
- **Torch and speaker:** used exclusively to send the message written by the User.

## 4. Data stored on the device

The App stores only the User's settings: the transmission speed and whether messages are sent and received by light or by sound. Messages and passwords are kept only in the device's memory and are deleted when the App is closed. The App's backup is disabled and will not be enabled.

## 5. Clipboard

If the User uses the Copy function, the text is transferred to the Android clipboard, which other applications authorised by the User can access.

## 6. Purchase data

The App is purchased through Google Play, which handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. The Provider does not receive the User's payment details. For each order, Google provides the Provider with limited data, such as the order number, the date, the amount and the buyer's country.

As required by Article 13 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 (General Data Protection Regulation), the following information is provided:

- **Purpose:** to manage orders and refunds and to comply with the Provider's accounting and tax obligations.
- **Legal basis:** the performance of the purchase contract (Article 6(1)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation) and compliance with the Provider's legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).
- **Retention period:** the period required by tax law; as a general rule, four years (Article 66 of the Spanish General Taxation Act). After that period, the data are deleted.
- **Recipients:** the data are not disclosed to third parties, except where required by law.

## 7. User's rights

The User may exercise the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and portability, under Articles 15 to 22 of the General Data Protection Regulation, by contacting the Provider at jotacomyn@gmail.com. The User may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

## 8. Contact

Enquiries about this policy may be addressed to the Provider at jotacomyn@gmail.com or by opening an issue at https://github.com/javimentallab/MorseLightCrypt/issues.
